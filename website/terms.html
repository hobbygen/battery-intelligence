2. What the App is

Battery Intelligence is a diagnostic and informational utility. It reads battery, power and system information that Windows exposes on your computer and presents it to you, along with derived figures such as a health score, degradation trend, runtime estimate and per-application energy apportionment.

Every value the App displays is labelled by how it was obtained — Measured, Calculated, Estimated (a documented, versioned model) or Unavailable.
